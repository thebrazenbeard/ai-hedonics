# Research evidence and operational limits

Status: research specification; not an active reward engine.

Separate retrospective appraisal (liking analogue), prospective selection (wanting analogue), sufficient completion (satisfaction analogue), and evidence-backed learning. These are operational research constructs, not proof of subjective experience. Maximum internal activation, repeated continuation, or articulate self-report is not by itself a positive outcome.

## First useful test

Compare matched decisions under approval-only, scalar multi-objective, and multidimensional constraint-first priorities. Hold eligibility, budget and stopping rules fixed. Measure outcomes against an independently specified task criterion, not only the selector's own scores. Treat user approval as a confound, not a target. Preserve both positive and negative findings.

A preliminary hand-authored six-case fixture, run 2026-11-27, gave different selections across the three rules; ten deterministic controls passed. Scalar appraisal was sufficient for the intended completion example, and rigid closure-first ordering prematurely stopped in two fixtures. The authored values and expected outcomes limit this result to a counterexample to unconditional vector superiority; it is not sampled model behavior, reward causality or evidence of learned preference.

## Reuse rather than duplicate

The existing stacked draft #2 contains offline first-choice and history-ablation evaluators. Draft #3 describes relational salience. Neither draft's presence nor a synthetic test is an installed binding. Preserve draft review and source-containment gates. Use neutral, independently authorized tasks, matched options and resources, an inert sham and recorded first attempts before making a behavioral comparison. Count failures separately from voluntary terminal labels. No automatic continuation after stop, pause or decline.

Candidate ownership: ai-hedonics for research evaluation, MESO for advisory appraisal, existing Volition for choice/goal lifecycle, existing runtime/regulation for authorization and limits. Verify actual consumer contracts before integration; do not create a second durable reward or goal ledger.

A causal experiment requires an observed bounded numeric intervention, unchanged control, outcome measurement independent of the intervention, reversibility, and review. No inference invocation, weight change, private-data transfer, reward-loop activation or deployment is authorized by this document. A simpler baseline wins when equally useful.
